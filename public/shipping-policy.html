1. Dispatch & Crafting Timelines

Unlike mass-produced fast fashion, NEYTHU STUDIO pieces are handmade by passionate artisans using slow-fashion crochet techniques. Our order preparation schedules depend on whether your piece is in-stock or custom-crafted:

Item Type Studio Dispatch Timeline Notes
Ready-to-Ship Pieces 1 to 2 Business Days (24 - 48 Hours) Pre-crafted inventory undergo final stitch quality check, steam finishing, and immediate packaging.
Made-to-Order & Custom Sizes 3 to 5 Business Days Hand-hooked to your exact measurements (bust/waist/length) from fresh yarn bundles by our artisan team.

*Please note: Orders are processed Monday through Saturday (excluding national holidays and regional bank holidays).

4. Packaging & Stitch Protection

Delicate crochet loops require special attention during transit. We ensure that every piece reaches you in immaculate condition:

  • Snag-Proof Wrapping: Each garment is individually swaddled in acid-free, breathable protective paper to prevent any snags, moisture buildup, or thread displacement.
  • Eco-Conscious Boxes: Orders are sealed in sturdy, recyclable cardboard boxes with tamper-evident security tape.
  • Care Card Included: Every shipment includes our signature Crochet Care & Washing Guide so you can cherish your piece for years.

5. Parcel Tracking & Notifications

You can stay informed at every step of your shipment journey:

  • Automated Dispatch SMS & Email: The moment your parcel is scanned by our logistics partner, you will receive an automated notification containing your Air Waybill (AWB) / Tracking Number.
  • Live Courier Tracking: Click the direct tracking link provided in your email or SMS to track the live location and expected delivery day.
  • Verified Courier Network: We partner with trusted logistics networks including Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, and India Post Speed Post.

6. Delivery Attempts & Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • Multiple Delivery Attempts: Our courier partners will attempt delivery up to three (3) consecutive times before returning the parcel to our atelier.
  • Delivery Coordination: The delivery associate will typically call or send an SMS before arriving at your location.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Please keep the exact payable cash ready at the time of delivery to avoid delivery delays. For security reasons, couriers cannot open packages prior to payment hand-off.

7. Address Accuracy & Updates

Please double-check your complete delivery address, post office, landmark, and 6-digit PIN code during checkout. If you notice an error in your address:

  • Before Dispatch: Contact us immediately at neythustudio@gmail.com, call our mobile helpline (+91 97449 11261), or message WhatsApp (+91 95440 93668) within 12 hours of placing the order, and we will update your shipping label free of charge.
  • After Dispatch: Once handed over to the courier, in-transit destination rerouting cannot be guaranteed and may cause transit delays.

8. Damaged, Tampered, or Lost Shipments

In the unlikely event that your parcel arrives with a broken seal or exterior package damage:

  • Do not accept parcels that appear visibly tampered with or ripped open.
  • If damage is discovered after opening, take clear photos and a brief unboxing video, and notify our team within 24 hours of delivery.
  • We will promptly arrange an artisan replacement as outlined in our Return & Refund Policy.

Need help tracking your shipment?

Our studio team can check live courier manifests or assist with delivery instructions.
