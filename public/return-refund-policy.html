Slow Fashion Policy

Return & Refund Policy

Due to the artisanal, handmade nature of our slow-fashion crochet creations, we maintain a strict No Return and No Refund Policy once an order is placed.

Last Updated: January 2026 All Sales Are Final
Privacy Policy Shipping Policy Return & Refund Policy Cancellation Policy Terms & Conditions Contact Information
All Sales Final Notice: Once an order is placed on NEYTHU STUDIO, we do not accept returns or issue refunds. Each piece is crafted loop-by-loop by individual artisans using natural yarn. Please review all garment measurements, colors, and descriptions carefully prior to checkout.

1. Strict No-Return & No-Refund Policy

At NEYTHU STUDIO, every single crochet garment, bag, and accessory is created through painstaking manual handcrafting. Because slow fashion requires dedicated artisan hours, delicate natural fibers, and made-to-order planning, all purchases are strictly final once placed.

We do not accept returns or process refunds under any of the following circumstances:

  • Change of mind, personal preference, or buyer's remorse after ordering.
  • Incorrect size chosen by the customer (please consult our sizing guides before ordering).
  • Natural variations in yarn shade, dye lot nuance, or handcrafted stitch tension (which are genuine hallmarks of authentic hand-hooked textile art).
  • Unclaimed, refused, or rejected parcels at the time of delivery.

2. Why We Do Not Accept Returns

Unlike mass-produced fast-fashion garments produced in automated factories, crochet cannot be replicated by any modern machine—every single stitch is looped by an artisan's hand:

  • Artisanal Time & Fiber Commitment: Once an order is entered, raw sustainable yarn skeins are allocated and hand-crocheting begins specifically for that order.
  • Delicate Fiber Preservation: Repeated transit, packaging, and handling can permanently stretch, snag, or distort delicate open-mesh and granny-square crochet stitches.
  • Hygiene & Slow Fashion Values: We guarantee that every customer receives a brand-new, freshly crafted, unhandled piece that has never been worn, washed, or tried on by anyone else.

3. Sole Exception: Transit Damage or Incorrect Item Delivered

The only exception to our No-Return Policy is if your parcel arrives physically damaged during courier transit, or if an entirely incorrect product was delivered in error:

Strict 24-Hour Reporting Window:

  • You must notify our studio within 24 hours of delivery. Any claims reported after 24 hours cannot be entertained.
  • You must provide 2-3 clear photographs and a short unboxing video showing the outer parcel packaging and the specific defect or wrong piece received.
  • Email the evidence along with your Order ID to neythustudio@gmail.com, call our helpline (+91 97449 11261), or message WhatsApp (+91 95440 93668).

Artisan Replacement Only: Upon verifying genuine courier damage or studio dispatch error, we will arrange a free reverse pickup and craft a replacement piece of the same item on priority. Monetary refunds are not provided.

4. Sizing & Fit Advisory Before Ordering

To ensure total satisfaction with your handcrafted piece, we strongly recommend taking the following steps before completing checkout:

  • Measure your bust, waist, and preferred garment length using a soft measuring tape.
  • Review the detailed dimensions and stitch elasticity notes on the product page.
  • Have questions about fit, yarn stretch, or styling? Contact our artisans via WhatsApp at +91 95440 93668 before placing your order—we are always happy to guide your size selection.

5. Proper Care & Longevity

Handmade crochet wear requires gentle love. We provide clear care instructions with every parcel:

  • Always hand wash gently in cold water with mild liquid wool/silk detergent.
  • Do not wring, machine wash, or tumble dry. Dry flat in shade to preserve stitch structure.
  • Store folded in a drawer rather than hung on wire hangers.
