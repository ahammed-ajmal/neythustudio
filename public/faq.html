Crochet Care & FAQ

Everything you need to know about caring for and wearing handmade crochet.

How do I wash and care for my handmade crochet pieces?

Always hand wash your crochet garments in cool water using a gentle, pH-neutral or wool-safe liquid detergent. Submerge gently without twisting or wringing. Press out excess water using a clean towel, reshape, and lay flat on a dry towel in the shade. Never hang wet crochet on hangers, as water weight can stretch the stitches.

Are all NEYTHU STUDIO pieces 100% handmade?

Yes, absolutely! Unlike knitting, true crochet cannot be replicated by factory machines. Every loop, petal, and granny square in our collection is hooked entirely by hand by skilled artisans. Slight natural variations in stitch tension are hallmarks of authentic handmade artistry.

What types of yarn and fibers do you use?

We work primarily with premium combed cotton, breathable linen blends, bamboo silk, and ultra-soft, non-scratchy wool blends. Our yarns are chosen for breathability, softness against sensitive skin, and long-term stitch retention.

Can I request custom sizes, custom colors, or bespoke commissions?

Yes! We adore custom commissions. If you need bespoke bust/waist measurements, longer/shorter hemlines, or a personalized colorway for our cardigans or totes, get in touch with us via our Contact page or WhatsApp.

Will crochet clothing stretch out over time?

Crochet stitches naturally relax and comfortably contour to your silhouette. To prevent unwanted elongation, always store your crochet tops, cardigans, and dresses neatly folded in a drawer or shelf—never hang them on hangers.

How long does shipping and handcrafting take?

Ready-to-ship pieces are dispatched within 1-2 business days. For made-to-order or custom sized items, our artisans take 3-5 days to meticulously hook your piece before dispatch. Standard courier delivery takes 3-7 business days across India.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for all orders across India, allowing you to pay cash safely at your doorstep upon receiving your parcel.

Can I cancel or return my order once placed?

Because every NEYTHU STUDIO piece involves slow-fashion handcrafting, immediate raw yarn allocation, and artisan queue scheduling, we operate under a strict No Cancellation and No Return Policy once an order is placed. All sales are final. The sole exception is physical transit damage or wrong item delivered, which must be reported with unboxing photos/video within 24 hours of delivery for a priority artisan replacement.
