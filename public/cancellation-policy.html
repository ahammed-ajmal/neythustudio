Cancellation Policy

Because all pieces are handcrafted by dedicated artisans upon order placement, we maintain a strict No Cancellation Policy once an order is placed.

Last Updated: January 2026 No Cancellations After Order

No Cancellation Notice: Once an order is placed on NEYTHU STUDIO, it cannot be cancelled, revoked, or modified. Yarn allocation, pattern sizing, and artisan time are committed immediately upon order confirmation. Please ensure your choice, sizing, and shipping address are verified before placing your order.

1. Strict No-Cancellation Policy

At NEYTHU STUDIO, we operate as a slow-fashion artisanal studio rather than a mass-production warehouse. When you place an order, our fulfillment system immediately enters your piece into our handcrafting and dispatch pipeline.

Consequently, we do not accept order cancellation requests once an order has been submitted. All confirmed orders will be processed, crafted, and dispatched for delivery to the provided shipping address.

2. Why Orders Cannot Be Cancelled

Unlike standard industrial apparel, authentic crochet requires human artisan hands for every loop:

  • Immediate Yarn Allocation: The required yarn skeins, specific dye lots, and natural fibers are measured and reserved for your garment as soon as an order is placed.
  • Artisan Time Commitment: Our artisans plan their weaving schedules and begin hand-crocheting to fulfill your order in a timely manner.
  • Fair Artisan Compensation: Our makers are fairly compensated for their craftsmanship and dedicated crafting hours, which cannot be undone once initiated.

3. Customer Commitment on Cash on Delivery (COD)

If you choose Cash on Delivery (COD) at checkout:

  • Selecting COD is a firm legal agreement to accept the parcel from the delivery associate and tender the payable invoice cash upon arrival.
  • Because orders cannot be cancelled after placement, parcels must not be rejected at the doorstep.
  • Refusing COD shipments causes unfair logistical costs to our artisanal studio and may lead to permanent suspension of COD privileges for your address/phone number.

4. Dispatched & In-Transit Orders

Once an order has been handed to our logistics courier network and an Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number has been issued, shipments cannot be halted, cancelled, or rerouted. The parcel will be delivered as scheduled according to our Shipping Policy.

5. Cancellations Initiated by NEYTHU STUDIO

In extremely rare scenarios, NEYTHU STUDIO reserves the right to cancel an order under specific operational constraints:

  • If a specific natural yarn dye lot fails our strict softness or colorfastness standards and cannot be replaced.
  • If a delivery PIN code is permanently unserviceable due to severe natural events or courier embargos.
  • In the event of an inadvertent technical pricing glitch or suspected fraudulent payment attempts.

If the studio must cancel your order, our artisans will contact you directly via phone or WhatsApp, and issue a 100% immediate full refund without any deductions.
