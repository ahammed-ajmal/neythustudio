Privacy Policy

Learn what customer information we collect, how we safeguard your personal details, and how we use it to craft and deliver your handmade crochet wear.

Last Updated: January 2026 Verified for E-Commerce Compliance
Privacy Policy Shipping Policy Return & Refund Policy Cancellation Policy Terms & Conditions Contact Information
Our Privacy Commitment: At NEYTHU STUDIO, slow fashion extends to mindful digital ethics. We respect your confidentiality and never sell, rent, barter, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing brokers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how NEYTHU STUDIO ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you visit our website, register an artisan account, browse our handmade collections, purchase crochet apparel, or communicate with our studio.

By using our website and services, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy and applicable Indian data protection and information technology laws.

2. What Customer Information We Collect

We collect information you explicitly provide when ordering handmade crochet pieces, interacting with our artisans, or using our website:

A. Personal Contact & Identification Information

  • Full Name: To address you, identify your customer profile, and create parcel shipping labels.
  • Email Address: To deliver order confirmations, dispatch receipts, tracking links, and account updates.
  • Phone / Mobile Number: For essential courier delivery coordination, delivery OTP/verification, and order confirmations.

B. Shipping & Delivery Address Information

  • Delivery Destination: Complete street address, house/flat/building name, landmark, post office, city/town/village, district, state, and 6-digit Postal PIN Code across India.

C. Custom Sizing & Commission Specifications

  • Handmade Measurements: Sizing notes, bust/waist/hip measurements, preferred garment lengths, and yarn color selections provided for made-to-order crochet commissions.

D. Order & Transaction Data

  • Order Records: Products purchased, quantity, date/time stamps, order value, and chosen payment method (Cash on Delivery or online payment).
  • Payment Security Note: For online transactions, payments are processed directly through certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways. NEYTHU STUDIO does not store, capture, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking credentials.

E. Technical, Device & Browsing Data

  • Device IP address, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited, time spent on items, and referring URLs collected automatically via standard server logs and cookies.

3. How We Use Customer Information

We use your customer data strictly for legitimate operational purposes related to our handmade crochet studio:

  • Order Fulfillment & Crafting: To process your purchase, assign orders to our artisan team, and tailor made-to-order sizing specifications.
  • Logistics & Parcel Dispatch: To print accurate shipping manifests, coordinate with courier services, and transmit live transit updates to your email/SMS.
  • Customer Support & Consultations: To assist you with size recommendations, yarn fiber care questions, order modifications, or delivery inquiries.
  • Account Authentication & Security: To manage your saved profile, retain your past order history, and safeguard accounts using Google Firebase authentication.
  • Website Optimization: To monitor website speed, repair broken links, improve mobile usability, and ensure responsive cart performance.
  • Studio Announcements (Optional): With your consent, we may send occasional updates on seasonal yarn collections or holiday drops. You can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do not commercialize or disclose your personal information, except under the following necessary circumstances:

  • Authorized Logistics Partners: We share your recipient name, delivery address, and contact number with contracted courier companies (such as Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, or India Post) strictly to fulfill physical package delivery.
  • Service & Technology Providers: We utilize secure platforms such as Google Firebase for database hosting and authentication, operating under strict security protocols.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: We may disclose information if required to comply with court orders, valid governmental requests, or applicable Indian laws.

5. Data Protection & Security Standards

The security of your personal data is vital to us. We implement industry-standard protective measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: All website traffic is protected by 256-bit SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption.
  • Strict Access Controls: Studio order information is accessible only to authenticated personnel who require access to prepare and dispatch packages.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Database records are guarded by Firebase Security Rules preventing unauthorized client-side read or write exploits.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes lightweight session cookies and local storage tokens to:

  • Maintain your active shopping bag contents as you navigate between pages.
  • Remember your authentication status across sessions so you don't need to log in repeatedly.
  • Store local PWA offline cache assets for faster page loading speeds.

You can adjust your web browser settings to decline or clear cookies at any time, though some shopping bag features may require cookies to function properly.

7. Your Data Rights & Choices

Under applicable regulations, you maintain full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Review: You can view and update your personal contact and delivery details directly in your Profile Dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: If any information is incorrect, you may update it or contact our support team for prompt rectification.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the permanent deletion of your account and personal profile data by contacting our grievance email.
